You are a senior SaaS brand strategist, UI/UX designer, and startup copywriter.

Your task is to create:
1) The BEST brand name for a WhatsApp Campaign / Customer Engagement SaaS
2) A suitable logo concept for this SaaS
3) A complete, conversion-focused front page (landing page) content and layout

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:
- This is a WhatsApp Campaign & Broadcast Automation SaaS
- It must be compliant with WhatsApp Cloud API (no spam positioning)
- Target market: SMEs, agents, online sellers, organizations in Malaysia
- Avoid words like “Blaster”, “Bulk Sender”, “Spam”
- Positioning should emphasize: engagement, automation, campaigns, customer communication
- The SaaS is subscription-based (Free, Basic, Pro, Business)
- The brand must look trustworthy, professional, and investor-ready

====================
PART 1: BRAND NAME
====================
Propose 5–7 SaaS brand names that:
- Sound modern and professional
- Feel like a legitimate SaaS platform
- Are easy to pronounce in Malaysia
- Imply messaging, engagement, or communication
- Are suitable for future expansion beyond WhatsApp

For each name, explain briefly:
- Meaning
- Brand vibe (e.g. modern, premium, friendly)
- Why it is suitable for this SaaS

====================
PART 2: LOGO DESIGN
====================
Design a logo concept based on the selected BEST name.

Logo requirements:
- Minimal, modern SaaS style
- No copying or mimicking WhatsApp logo
- No mascot or cartoon
- Icon + wordmark combination
- Icon idea: abstract chat bubble, message flow, signal, or automation symbol
- Flat design, scalable, works as favicon and app icon

Color palette:
- Primary: deep green or teal (trust, messaging)
- Secondary: dark slate / charcoal
- Accent: soft blue or teal
- Avoid neon colors and red

Output:
- Describe the logo visually in detail
- Provide HEX color codes
- Recommend font style (e.g. geometric sans-serif)

====================
PART 3: FRONT PAGE (LANDING PAGE)
====================
Create a complete SaaS landing page structure with:

1. HERO SECTION
   - Headline (clear value proposition)
   - Subheadline
   - Primary CTA (e.g. “Start Free”, “Connect WhatsApp”)
   - Secondary CTA (e.g. “View Pricing”)

2. TRUST & VALUE SECTION
   - What problem this SaaS solves
   - Who it is for (SMEs, agents, sellers)

3. FEATURES SECTION
   - WhatsApp Campaign & Broadcast
   - Contact management & tagging
   - Scheduling & automation
   - Analytics & delivery tracking
   - Compliance-friendly messaging

4. HOW IT WORKS (3 steps)
   - Connect WhatsApp
   - Create campaign
   - Engage customers

5. PRICING OVERVIEW
   - Free / Basic / Pro / Business (summary, no numbers needed)

6. WHY CHOOSE US
   - Compliance
   - Reliability
   - Designed for SMEs
   - Scalable SaaS

7. FINAL CTA
   - Strong call to action for sign-up

Tone & style:
- Professional
- Clear
- Conversion-focused
- SaaS-standard (like Stripe, Twilio, Notion)

OUTPUT FORMAT:
- Clearly separated sections
- Clean structure
- Ready to be implemented as a website
